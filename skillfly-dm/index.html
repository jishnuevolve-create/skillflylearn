Questions Beginners Ask Us

Do I need any prior experience to join? +

No. Both programs start from fundamentals, so complete beginners and final-year students can follow along.

What is included in the program fee? +

All live classes, downloadable templates and worksheets, lifetime access to session recordings, mentor doubt support, a capstone project review and your completion certificate.

Are classes online or offline? +

Both. Every program runs as live online batches and offline classroom batches, and recordings are available either way.

How long are the programs? +

Digital Marketing runs for 3 months and HR Management for 6 months, with weekday and weekend batch options.

Will I get a certificate and placement help? +

Yes — a shareable SkillFly completion certificate, plus resume review, interview preparation and referrals to hiring partners.
